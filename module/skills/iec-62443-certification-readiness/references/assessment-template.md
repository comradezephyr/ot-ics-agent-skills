# IEC 62443 Certification Readiness: <product, system, or service>

Readiness status: <ready | ready with conditions | not ready>
Scope owner: <name>

## 1. Scope Statement

Per part in scope: part number, audited target, version, and boundary. The audited target for 4-1 is the development process, for 4-2 the component at a specific version, for 3-3 the system and its zone boundary, for 2-4 the service delivery process.

## 2. Prerequisite Check

Per part: required designs and processes, present or absent, with references. Zone and conduit design for 3-3, requirement mapping for 4-2, lifecycle documentation for 4-1, service delivery processes for 2-4.

## 3. Evidence Register

Per part and per requirement family (SM-1 through SM-13 for 4-1, CR 1.1 through CR 7.8 for 4-2, SR families at target SL for 3-3, service provider families for 2-4):

| Requirement Family | Evidence Artifact | State (met, partial, missing) | Owner |

## 4. Component Certificates

| Component | Certificate Part | Certificate Scope | Version | What It Does Not Cover |

## 5. Gap Analysis

| Part | Gap | Severity (High, Medium, Low) | Remediation | Owner | Effort |

## 6. Certification Body Record

Body, accreditation status, pre-assessment option and cost, audit format, remediation window between stages.

## 7. Readiness Decision

Decision, date, decision makers, and the cost of both delay paths.

## 8. Prohibited Patterns

System claims from component certificates, scope without an audited target, evidence registers without named artifacts, gaps without severity, readiness calls without named decision makers.
