# IR Plan Review Questionnaire

Answer per plan under review. Record evidence for every answer.

## Plan Structure

1. Which document is the OT IR plan, what version, and when was it last updated?
2. Does the plan follow a recognized structure (800-61 phases, or a documented equivalent)?
3. What playbooks exist? List titles.

## Authority and Safety

4. Who is the named decision authority for isolation, shutdown, and notification?
5. Does the plan state that safety outranks containment, and where?
6. What safety checks are attached to each containment action?

## Escalation and Contacts

7. Where is the contact list, and when was it last verified against reality?
8. What are the after-hours paths for operations, safety, and IT?
9. What regulator or customer notification obligations exist, and who owns them?

## Playbooks

10. Which incident types have playbooks, and which top types are missing?
11. When was each playbook last used or exercised?
12. Do playbooks name their decision authority and safety constraint?

## Post-Incident

13. What is the post-incident review process, and who owns it?
14. Where are past review outputs, and what changed as a result?

## Integration

15. What does the enterprise IR plan say about OT, and what does the OT plan say about the enterprise?
16. Which enterprise actions require operations review before touching OT?
17. How do the two escalation paths connect during a joint incident?
