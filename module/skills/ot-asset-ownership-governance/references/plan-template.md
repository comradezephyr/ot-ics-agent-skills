## 1. Ownership Matrix

| Asset Class | Operational Owner | Security Counterpart | Change Approval Authority | Access Review Owner | Lifecycle Sign-off |

Asset classes: controllers and logic, HMIs, EWS, network devices, historians, DMZ services, SIS.

## 2. Change Approval Alignment

Per class: the gate named in the change workflow, and confirmation it matches this matrix.

## 3. Access Review Design

Per class: quarterly review scope, record format, and owner.

## 4. Lifecycle Sign-off

Per class: who signs end-of-life, replacement, and decommission decisions, with the safety engineer co-sign rule.

## 5. Unowned Asset Escalation

Deadline, what untrusted means operationally, and the resolution path.

## 6. Dispute Resolution

Resolver, decision basis (consequence statement per zone), and recorded outcomes.

## 7. Review Record

Executive sign-off date, matrix review cadence, and change history of the governance document itself.

## 8. Prohibited Patterns

Departments instead of named owners, ownership and change workflow naming different approvers, access reviews with no owner or record format, SIS lifecycle signed without the safety engineer, unowned assets with no operational consequence.
